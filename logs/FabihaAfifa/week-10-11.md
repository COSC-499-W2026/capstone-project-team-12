## A13: Daily streaks and challenges

**Requirement and design.** Students need to see their streak and daily challenges, earn rewards as they play, and receive a mix of goals. I split the work into data models and streak tracking, challenge generation, progress and rewards, milestone rewards, the API, and weighted target selection.

**Review and tests.** Mia flagged the hard-coded Vancouver timezone and the mocked database in the streak tests. I switched to the app's timezone setting and rewrote the tests to use real database rows and assert the saved active date. This let me verify the state that the challenge and streak API reads. [#356] [#357] [#359] [#360] [#382] [#384]

## A14: Classroom leaderboard

**Requirement and design.** Students need rankings based on points earned in their class. I used the existing points ledger to exclude Practice and other classes, include students with zero points, and give equal scores the same rank.

**Review and tests.** Mia pointed out that the leaderboard exposed student IDs to classmates. I changed the response to show usernames and an `is_me` flag so the frontend can highlight the caller. The gameplay integration test checks that scoring a location updates the leaderboard through the normal scoring flow. [#362] [#363] [#365]

## A16: Classroom analytics and participation

**Requirement and design.** Teachers and assigned TAs need class totals, location outcomes, individual student statistics, difficulty hints, and participation grades. I split the work into location and student analytics, difficulty recommendations, participation calculations, and CSV export.

**Review and tests.** Review caught missing class-level totals, so I added them and moved aggregation into SQL. The CSV review caught missing columns, omitted students with zero activity, and an archive-status bug that could overwrite the reported status of students who had left or been removed. I added regression tests for those cases and for formula-like usernames in CSV files. The export reached 565 changed lines, and the reviewer accepted the exception. Each of the 12 new code PRs stays below 500 changed lines and 10 files relative to its target branch. [#367] [#369] [#370] [#371] [#372]

## A12: Remaining achievements and personal statistics

**Integration.** Eddy's A10 photo finalization and upload PRs merged on November 18 and now call the scoring helper I added in #300. With that integration in place, I opened four A12 follow-up PRs. [#300] [#346] [#387]

- **Challenge Master.** I added a per-language unlock at ten challenging completions and exposed progress in the achievements API. The test checks that the badge stays locked at nine and unlocks once on the tenth. [#428]
- **Perfect Session.** I added the unlock for completing all five live locations in one session. The tests cover failed and skipped locations and verify that a row replaced by a shuffle is excluded. I corrected the replay test to expect `SESSION_NOT_ACTIVE` after the game ends. [#429]
- **Campus Master.** I reused the game draw's eligibility rules and counted distinct completions in the requested language across difficulties. The test checks that repeat visits, failed results, and other players' completions do not fill gaps in coverage. [#430]
- **Personal statistics.** I added `GET /me/statistics` after finding it in the technical spec. It reports the signed-in player's completed locations, completion rate, and average time by language and difficulty. Tests cover classroom and Practice results, player isolation, and the 30-minute cap per timed completion. [#432]
